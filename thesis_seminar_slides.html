Augmenting Text Prompts with
Spatial Proxy Controls in
AI-Assisted 3D Scene Generation within Unity

Are coarse geometry and region masks better control channels than language for spatial creative work?

Luojinxuan Wang
Advisor: Dr. Michael Schmitz · Experimental Media Lab, HBKsaar
Department of Computer Science · July 2026
Problem statement

A story every studio knows by now

1 · The wish

A designer asks AI to fill a blockout.

2 · The render

Visually plausible. Right style, right mood.

3 · In the engine

Intersections. Wrong scale. Blocked path.

The missing information is spatial. The prompt cannot carry it precisely.

Problem statement

Text carries semantics, not geometry

Language is good at

Identity · style · mood
“medieval market”

Language is poor at

Distance · rotation · boundaries
“near” is qualitative, not metric [1,2]

Model guesses→ Prompt iteration→ Gulf of execution [5]
The research gap

Models accept spatial conditions [7–9] — but designers still lack an in-engine workflow for authoring them.

Motivation

Designers already have the right pipeline — keep it

Blockout + mixed-initiative workflow [3,4]

Research questions

One question, split across the creative loop

Do spatial proxies improve control of AI-assisted 3D creation in Unity?
RQ1 · Creation stage

Primitive proxies vs. text

Better spatial fidelity? Lower mental effort?

RQ2 · Refinement stage

3D region mask vs. regeneration

More local control? Better scene preservation?

The missing layer

Implementation · Creation architecture

From spatial proxy to a fitted mesh

Design principle: spatial constraints come from geometry; the prompt names the subject and visual idiom.

Implementation · Single-view handoff

The source image is a specification, not a beauty render

The correction is validated on actual generated GLBs; clean synthetic boxes hid the failure modes.

Implementation · Refinement architecture

Four views inform one local replacement

This is region replacement, not masked latent editing of an existing 3D representation.

Implementation · Refinement locality

Unity makes the generated change spatially local

Guarantee

Hard geometric locality + welded transition. Remaining boundary: unchanged exterior samples do not condition the Hunyuan shape latent.
